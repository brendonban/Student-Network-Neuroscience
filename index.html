Who We Are

Our Mission

In March 2025, the Student Network for Neuroscience was launched with the goal of creating a vibrant and dynamic community of students in the field of neuroscience. We are a platform that fosters exchange, expands individual opportunities, and drives collaboration in this fascinating field.

As part of the Psychology Student Council Conference (PsyFaKo e.V.), we are a network that supports student initiatives and promotes exchange at the European level. We pursue an interdisciplinary approach and explicitly address students from various disciplines – including students of medicine, biology, computer science, philosophy, and various other related fields. We are convinced that interdisciplinary communication and education are essential for the field of neuroscience.

What awaits you with us? We offer you a variety of opportunities to network, expand your knowledge, and advance your career. Make valuable contacts with other students, researchers, and professionals, receive exclusive information about current opportunities and events, and exchange ideas with other members on exciting topics. We organize regular events that provide insights into various research areas as well. Our main focus however lies in assisting the realization and promotion of student-led research and educational projects. For this, we provide various resources – for example contacts to individuals in the relevant (research) fields.

Depending on the project, there may be differences in the resources and possibilities. Our main communication platform is Discord, where you can exchange ideas with other members, participate in discussions, and receive up-to-date information. The communication is almost exclusively in English.

We look forward to getting to know you and working with you to shape the future of neuroscience.
